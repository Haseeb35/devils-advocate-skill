# Quick mode: annual vs monthly subscription

> **Prompt:** quick gut check: should I buy the annual plan for our design tool instead of monthly? saves 20% and we've used it for 8 months

<sub>Unedited output from Claude Code with the skill installed.</sub>

😈 **Biggest risk:** You pay for 12 months of seats up front, but most annual plans won't let you cut seats until renewal. If the team shrinks or you switch tools (say, you consolidate onto something you already pay for), the 20% you saved is gone.

**Best argument for:** You've used it for 8 months, so you clearly depend on it. 20% off is about 2.4 months free each year. Unless you'd drop it within roughly 10 months, annual comes out cheaper.

**Verdict: ⚠️ Go, with changes.** Buy annual, but only for the seats you're confident you'll still need in 12 months. Keep any extra seats on monthly if the tool lets you, and put the renewal date in your calendar.

**Ask yourself:** Is there any real chance you'll change tools or cut headcount in the next 10 months?
